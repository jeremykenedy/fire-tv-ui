Switch 2, Switch Lite, Xbox Series X, DVD, Blu-ray, LaserDisc, Meta, and Meta
Quest vectors are from Wikimedia Commons. Individual source links and source
SHA-256 values are recorded in catalog.json.

The VCR, stereo receiver, MacBook, laptop, and PC pictograms and the shared tile
layout are original project artwork covered by the repository's MIT license.

Denon, Yamaha, Onkyo, Marantz, Bose, and Klipsch vectors are from their official
websites. Vizio is the unmodified inline vector from its official website.
Sony, Kenwood, Pioneer, and Harman Kardon vectors are from Wikimedia Commons.
Insignia is from Iconape. JBL, Sonos, Samsung, and LG vectors are from the
Simple Icons project (CC0), pinned to revision:
90646e4bf7f3b89df6c37e65baf3e7bc759a517c
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

Exact source URLs and SHA-256 values for every audio brand are in catalog.json.
The shared renderer applies a monochrome treatment without changing source files.
Third-party brand marks remain the property of their respective owners; the
project's MIT license applies to the tile layout, not to those trademarks.
