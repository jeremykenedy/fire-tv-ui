The primary upload explicitly states Creative Commons Attribution reuse allowed.
This is an edited loop of real flight, not a naturally closed camera path.
Source lighting, cloud cover and camera banking are retained. Playback speed
changes timing; it does not add unique source frames. The film has 25 unique source frames per second. Slowing playback
retains those frames for longer. No music or other source audio is included.

This film is an ambient backdrop; it does not depict the selected city or current forecast.
