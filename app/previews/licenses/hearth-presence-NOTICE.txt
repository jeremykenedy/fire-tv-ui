Adapted by slight desaturation, H.264 transcoding and loop-boundary blending.
The wood flame-height backdrop is a temporal minimum of this licensed footage.
Its alpha channel records the fixed log silhouette. Only the separately sampled
moving flame emission changes height; the recorded logs keep their geometry.
Small and large wood videos bake that height adaptation at 1920x1080, 30 fps;
medium uses the original bundled wood video. The backdrop and adapted videos
retain the same CC BY 3.0 attribution and license.

above (CC BY 3.0) with an original rendered black-steel burner and glass bed
(Apache-2.0). Changes include temporal background subtraction, removal of fuel
and room pixels, independent time offsets, additive compositing and loop blending.
