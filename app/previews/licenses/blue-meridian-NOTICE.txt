Bundled Earth imagery is derived from NASA source material:

Blue Marble Next Generation, July 2004 base map:
Reto Stockli, NASA Earth Observatory / NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/

Blue Marble Clouds, 2002:
Reto Stockli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center; MODIS Atmosphere Group.
https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57747/blue-marble-clouds/77558l

Black Marble, 2012 night-light composite:
NASA Earth Observatory, NOAA National Geophysical Data Center,
and Suomi National Polar-orbiting Partnership VIIRS data.
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/

The source images were resized, converted to JPEG, and stripped of metadata.
The globe combines archival images from different years for visual display.
It is not current weather or a scientific observation of one moment.

NASA material is used under its Images and Media Usage Guidelines:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
NASA does not endorse this application.

Exact source URLs, source hashes, and shipped hashes are recorded in
assets/SOURCES.json.
