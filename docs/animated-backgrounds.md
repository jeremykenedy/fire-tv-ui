# Animated menu backgrounds

Open Settings > Animated backgrounds. Choose Quiet particles, Aurora ribbons, Connected lights or Prism light. Speed offers 25%, 50% and 100%; intensity and brightness offer 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%.

Animation is optional and defaults to Off. Off uses your existing wallpaper without replacing its saved choice. These native effects run behind Main, stop while another screen is open and resume when Main returns. They do not reset the TV inactivity timer. Prism light is a separate menu effect, not the Prism Clock screensaver.

Style and controls are included in portable launcher backups. Existing installations retain their wallpaper and start with animation Off until a style is chosen.
