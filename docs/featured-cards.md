# Featured cards

Open Preferences > UI settings > Cards > Featured card setup. Existing card and appearance controls remain available below it.

## Choose cards

Enable cards, choose Fit or Fill artwork, and select which published sources appear. Hiding a source affects its cards only, not its app tile. Refresh queries the actual available feeds. Reload home cards closes setup and rebuilds Home.

Focus a card on Home and press the remote Menu button. Card options can hide that exact item, open its feed for ordering, or choose feeds and card settings.

Channel Manager offers Manage cards under each channel. Right or Menu on a channel also opens its individual cards. Hide or restore one card without disabling the channel. Move up or Move down changes the item order within that feed, and Home rotates the visible items in your saved order within that feed's fixed card. Newly published items follow your saved items; removed items are ignored. Restore all affects only that channel. The separate Hidden cards screen restores individual cards or all hidden cards. Hidden choices are included in portable launcher backups; publisher rows and watch history remain unchanged.

Each app or logical channel keeps one fixed card on Home. Its visible titles rotate internally at the saved 5, 8, 15 or 30 second interval, or a custom whole-second interval from 1 to 86400; Manual stops automatic changes. The whole strip no longer advances automatically. The selected child remains in place across refresh when it is still available. Opening its menu or details holds that exact child so actions do not target a later title. Next title and Previous title change only that feed.

The same saved interval also controls Big picture artwork. Changing it applies when you return from settings. The focused card supplies its current child artwork when that wallpaper mode is selected. Existing item visibility, item order, feed order and source choices are preserved.

## Arrange top-level feeds

Choose Arrange top-level cards in Featured card setup or the focused card Menu. Move left and Move right reorder the Plex, IPTV and other feed cards on Home. This is separate from Move up and Move down inside a feed. Reset top-level order restores the normal feed sequence. Order choices are included in portable backups.

## App recommendations

App recommendation cards are optional and require Android notification access. Enable the option and open Manage notification access to grant it explicitly. The launcher accepts recommendation notifications from supported installed apps, including Netflix, Prime Video, Live TV, News, YouTube, Spotify and Tubi. A listed app may publish no recommendations on a particular TV.

Cards use the app's original action. Other notification categories and actions created by a different package are ignored. Notification access and private recommendation artwork stay on that TV. URI artwork is read only through the producer's normal content-provider access or a reviewed public HTTPS image host, with bounded downloads and image dimensions. Turning the option off or removing access withdraws its cards.

System Watch Next, Android preview channels and app recommendation notifications are separate sources. Source status distinguishes empty feeds from unavailable access. Amazon catalog integration does not automatically provide a feed to a third party launcher.

## Personal Plex server

Connect your server in Plex setup and approve the displayed code at plex.tv/link. Credentials are encrypted in device storage and excluded from portable backups. Existing connections can recheck their approved registration without requesting another code.

Feeds and users provides Continue Watching, New movies, New TV episodes, Currently playing and Recently watched. Continue Watching belongs to the authorized account. Activity feeds can filter one or more users actually visible to that account. Other users' Continue Watching opens a separate Plex Home profile picker: select an authorized profile to add its own feed, or remove it later. Protected profiles require their own PIN. Each profile uses a separately authorized server credential; activity history is never presented as another user's Continue Watching. The server's permissions determine which sessions and history are available.

Choose a requested card count per feed. The combined cache limits the effective total to 24 cards. Disabling a feed removes its cards after the next successful update. A failed request preserves the prior working connection.

Opening a personal card validates its server and media identity. The item screen shows available saved progress and one Open item in Plex action. It does not offer an unverified automatic Resume action. Plex may request a household profile selection before opening the item. The installed Plex app may consume an external item request while selecting a household profile and return to Plex Home. Automatic card-to-item handoff and offset resume are not verified on that version. The original TV check confirmed playback only after manually choosing the matching Continue Watching item inside Plex. Other installed Plex versions may behave differently.
